Our story

Running a busy workshop on paper means lost job cards, double-booked bays and customers who have to phone to find out whether their car is ready. ARMS was built to fix that. It puts the whole life of a repair in one place, from the first online booking to the paid invoice, so everyone works from the same up-to-date record.

For customers, that means booking without an account, seeing prices before committing and following the repair live. For our staff, it means less admin and more time spent on the cars themselves.

  • 20 half-hour booking slots every day
  • 6 repair stages you can track online
  • 0 accounts needed to book

How we work

Customers book online without an account: choose a service, pick a half-hour slot between 08:00 and 17:30, and our reception team reviews and confirms the request. When the vehicle arrives it is checked in, a technician is assigned, and every inspection and part used against the job is recorded. Customers follow the whole repair with nothing but the booking reference they received at confirmation time.

A short history

  1. The paper years

    Two bays, a diary and paper job cards. Slot clashes and lost cards were a weekly event, and customers phoned the front desk just to ask whether their car was ready.

  2. Online booking arrives

    Customers could finally request a slot without calling. Bookings arrive as pending requests that reception confirms, so the diary is never double-booked again.

  3. The workshop goes digital

    Vehicle check-in, bay allocation, technician assignment and inspections moved into the system, with the parts catalogue and usage tracking following.

  4. One record, end to end

    Today the invoice, the payment record and the public status page all hang off the same job. A customer with a booking reference can see the whole story without a single phone call.
