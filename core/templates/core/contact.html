Lost your booking reference?

The repair status page needs the reference we gave you at confirmation. If you have lost it, call the workshop and we will look up your booking for you.

Cancelling or changing a booking

Plans change. Call or visit the workshop and reception can cancel a pending or confirmed appointment. To move to a different time, cancel the existing booking and make a new one for the slot you want. Letting us know early frees the slot for another customer.

Frequently asked questions

When is my slot actually held for me?

Once reception accepts your request. Until then the booking is pending: the slot you picked is a request, not a reservation, and reception confirms it against the workshop diary.

Can I book for someone else?

Yes. Give the driver's name and phone number on the booking form, and hand the booking reference to whoever brings the car in. Anyone holding the reference can follow the repair and view the invoice.

Do I have to bring paperwork for my car?

No. The booking form already captures the model and registration, and reception confirms the details with you at check-in. Just bring the car and the booking reference.

Can I get an update by phone instead of online?

Of course. The public status page is there for convenience, not as a requirement. Call the workshop with your booking reference and reception will tell you exactly where the job stands.

What if my repair needs parts you don't have in stock?

The parts catalogue tracks stock, and the workshop is warned when something runs low. If a part still has to be ordered for your job, the technician records it against the repair and it appears on your invoice - we will tell you when it affects the completion date.

Can I pay by card?

Not at the moment. Payment is cash only, taken at the reception desk when you collect the car; the invoice is marked paid once reception has taken it.
