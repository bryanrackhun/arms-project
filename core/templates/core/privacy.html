The short version

We collect only what we need to book and repair your car. You never create an account, staff see only what their role requires, and the public status page shows progress, not personal or internal details.

What we collect

When you make an online booking we ask for your name, a phone number or email address (or both), your car's model and registration number, and the service and time you want. Any notes you add are stored with the booking. Reception confirms or corrects the vehicle details when you arrive.

While your car is with us, the workshop also records the details of the job itself: the technician assigned, inspection findings, parts used, status changes and the final invoice.

How we use it

  • {% include "core/_pub_icon.html" with name="check" %}To review and confirm your appointment, and contact you about it.
  • {% include "core/_pub_icon.html" with name="check" %}To identify your vehicle at check-in and carry out the repair.
  • {% include "core/_pub_icon.html" with name="check" %}To show you the progress of your repair and prepare your invoice.
  • {% include "core/_pub_icon.html" with name="check" %}To keep an accurate service history, so we can answer later questions about the work.

No customer accounts

You do not create an account with us. The booking reference we issue when the appointment is confirmed is the only credential the public pages ask for. Anyone holding that reference can see the repair's progress, so treat it as you would a receipt.

Who inside the workshop sees your data

Access is limited by role. Reception staff handle bookings, check-ins and invoices; technicians see the jobs assigned to them and record inspections and parts used; administrators manage the staff, catalogue and payroll. Staff sign in with their own accounts, and screens outside a person's role are refused.

The public status page

The public status page deliberately shows no staff names, internal notes or itemised costs. It reports only the stage your repair has reached - booked, checked in, job assigned, inspected, in progress or completed - and, once billed, your invoice.

Invoices and payment

When a repair is completed and billed, the invoice records the payment method you choose. The bill is visible through the same booking reference, on the public status page. Payment itself is taken in cash at the counter; we do not collect or store card details.

Sharing

Your booking and repair records are used by the workshop to serve you. We do not sell them or pass them to third parties for marketing.

The only people outside the workshop who can see anything about your job are people you give the booking reference to. The reference is the credential: the public pages ask for nothing else and show no more to whoever holds it than what they show you.

How long we keep it

A booking that is cancelled before check-in leaves behind only the appointment record, so the slot's history stays honest. A completed repair - the vehicle, the job, its inspections, parts and invoice - is kept as your service history, so a later question about the work has an answer in the record rather than in someone's memory. If you would rather we did not keep a historical record, contact the workshop and we will talk it through.

How we protect it

  • {% include "core/_pub_icon.html" with name="check" %}Accounts for staff only. Every staff member signs in with their own account; there is no shared login.
  • {% include "core/_pub_icon.html" with name="check" %}Role-checked screens. A page outside a person's role is refused by the system, not by convention.
  • {% include "core/_pub_icon.html" with name="check" %}Per-job limits for technicians. A technician can act only on the jobs assigned to them.
  • {% include "core/_pub_icon.html" with name="check" %}Public pages show progress, not records. No staff names, internal notes or itemised costs reach the status page.

Staff sessions expire after 30 minutes of inactivity, so an unattended desk does not leave the system open.

Your choices

You choose how we reach you: the booking form needs a phone number or an email address, not both. If you want to check or correct the details we hold, contact the workshop.

Questions

If anything here is unclear, contact the workshop and we will walk you through it.
