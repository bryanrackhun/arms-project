How it works

Get serviced in 3 steps

  1. 1 Choose a service

  2. 2 Pick an available time slot

Your repair, stage by stage

What happens after you book

  1. Booked

    Your request is reviewed and your slot confirmed. You receive a booking reference.

  2. Vehicle checked in

    Reception confirms your car's details when you arrive at the workshop.

  3. Job assigned

    A technician qualified for your service is assigned and takes on the job.

  4. Vehicle inspected

    The technician inspects the car and records what they find before work begins.

  5. In progress

    The repair is under way. Any parts fitted are logged against your job.

  6. Completed

    Work is done and your invoice is ready to view and settle using your reference.

FAQ

Common questions

Do I need an account to book?

No. Booking is open to everyone. Choose a service and a time, leave your name, a way to reach you and your car's model and registration, and you are done. Your booking reference is all you need afterwards.

Is my appointment confirmed straight away?

An online booking starts as a pending request. Our reception team checks it against the workshop schedule and confirms it, and from then on the slot is held for you.

What do I need to bring?

Just your car and your booking reference. We already have your car's model and registration from the booking, and reception confirms them with you when you check in.

How much will my repair cost?

Each service shows its base cost in {{ currency }} before you book. The final invoice covers that labour plus any parts the technician had to fit, and you can view it with your booking reference.

How do I pay?

Once the repair is completed and billed, you choose your payment method on the invoice page. The workshop currently accepts cash, settled at the reception desk when you collect your car.

Can I cancel or change my booking?

Yes. Call or visit the workshop and reception can cancel a pending or confirmed appointment. To move to another time, cancel and make a new booking for the slot you want.

I lost my booking reference. What now?

Contact the workshop and give us your name and booking details. We will look up your booking and pass the reference back to you.
