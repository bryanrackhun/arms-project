In brief

Your booking is confirmed once our team accepts it. Arrive on time with your reference, follow the repair online, and pay the invoice in cash at the counter when the work is done.

1. Bookings are requests

An online booking is submitted as pending. It becomes a confirmed appointment only once our team accepts it. The slot you pick is a request, not a guarantee.

2. Appointment times

Appointments run on the half hour between 08:00 and 17:30. A slot must be in the future and not already held by another active appointment; the booking form will tell you if the time you picked is taken.

3. Your details

When booking you give your name, a phone number or email address, and your car's model and registration number. Please make sure these are accurate. We use them to contact you and identify your vehicle, and reception confirms them with you at check-in.

4. Check-in and cancellation

Please arrive at your confirmed time so reception can check your vehicle in. If you cannot make it, call the workshop - a pending or confirmed appointment can be cancelled. An appointment you miss without telling us may be recorded as a no-show, and the slot is released to other customers.

5. The repair

After check-in, a technician is assigned and the job moves through not started, in progress and completed. Each stage is visible on the public status page using your booking reference. If further work or parts are needed, the workshop records them against the job and they appear on the invoice.

6. Pricing and payment

Service base prices and estimated durations are shown in the catalogue before you book, and all amounts are quoted in Myanmar Kyat (MMK). The final invoice covers the labour and any parts used, plus any applicable tax shown on the invoice. Estimated durations are a guide, not a promise.

Once the repair is completed and billed, you choose your payment method on the invoice page. The workshop currently accepts cash only, paid at the counter; the invoice is marked paid once reception has taken your payment.

7. Your booking reference

Your booking reference is how you follow the repair and view the final bill. Anyone who has it can see your repair's progress and invoice, so keep it private and share it only with people you trust to act for you.

8. Workmanship and parts

Every inspection finding, part drawn and status change is recorded against your job, so if something is not right, bring the booking reference back to the workshop: the record of exactly what was done and fitted is the starting point for putting it right. Fitted parts are the parts recorded on your invoice - the record and the bill are one and the same.

9. Liability

The workshop takes care of your vehicle while it is checked in for the booked work. Vehicles must be collected during opening hours once the repair is completed. We are not responsible for personal items left in the vehicle - please take valuables with you when you drop it off. Nothing in these terms limits anything that cannot be limited by law.

10. Changes to these terms

We may update these terms as the workshop's services change. The version on this page is the one that applies to new bookings.

11. Contact

Any question about these terms or a specific job - contact the workshop.
